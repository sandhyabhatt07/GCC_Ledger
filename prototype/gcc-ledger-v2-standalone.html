    How the likelihood score works

    Judgement-set weights, not trained

    Likelihood is our estimate of the chance a GCC becomes operational within 12 months. Each documented step on the ladder adds its weight to a starting score, and the logistic function turns the total into a percentage. A step that is not checked counts as zero, so filling in filings can only raise a score.

    Once a few years of entities have known outcomes, these weights would be fitted by logistic regression on that history and checked for calibration, instead of being set by hand.
